\section{Appendix F: Plasmonic Nano-Geometry
Sweep}\label{sec:app_plasmonic_geometry}

\subsection{Objective}\label{objective}

Comprehensive plasmonic nanostructure analysis: frequency-dependent
permittivity (Drude), Mie scattering, coupled‑dipole near‑field
interactions, geometry optimization, and field‑enhancement mapping for
receptor‑scale enhancement.

\subsection{Methods (src)}\label{methods-src}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \passthrough{\lstinline!src/case\_studies/plasmonic\_geometry.py!}

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!drude\_model\_permittivity(frequency\_hz, metal\_type)!}
    --- material permittivity model
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!mie\_scattering\_sphere(radius\_m, wavelength\_m, eps\_particle, eps\_medium)!}
    --- Mie solutions
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!coupled\_dipoles\_near\_field(positions, polarizabilities, wavelength)!}
    --- multi‑particle interactions
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!optimize\_plasmonic\_geometry(wavelength\_range, constraints)!}
    --- geometry optimization
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!field\_distribution\_near\_particle(particle\_params, grid\_points)!}
    --- near‑field maps
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!sweep\_plasmonic\_quality(radii\_m, metal\_eps, medium\_eps)!}
    --- parameter sweeps for Q‑factor analysis
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
