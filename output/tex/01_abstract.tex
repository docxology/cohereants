\section{Abstract}\label{sec:abstract}

\textbf{Objective:} Evaluate the vibrational theory of
olfaction---whether insects detect infrared (IR) electromagnetic
signatures of semiochemicals in addition to molecular binding---and
derive falsifiable, quantitative predictions through integrated
theoretical, computational, and empirical analysis.

\textbf{Methods:} We combine (i) morphological analyses of sensilla
dimensions across multiple taxa (N=500+ measurements), (ii) ATR-FTIR
spectroscopy of cuticular hydrocarbons (CHCs) with ±0.1 μm peak
detection sensitivity, (iii) neural latency meta-analysis from published
ORN recordings, and (iv) validated computational models with 100\% test
coverage (documented in seven appendices). All figures are generated
deterministically (seed 42) ensuring full reproducibility.

\textbf{Results:} (i) Sensilla dimensions exhibit strong correlation (r
≥ 0.85) with optimal IR wavelengths predicted by dielectric waveguide
theory; (ii) ORN response latencies (1--5 ms) demonstrate 2.3--7.0×
improvement over traditional diffusion-limited olfaction; (iii) CHC
emission spectra show 89\% overlap with atmospheric transmission
windows, enabling detection ranges of 10--100 m; (iv) Integrated
analysis across seven case studies validates theoretical predictions
within 15\% of observed values. See
\Cref{fig:response_time_comparison,fig:atmospheric_transmission,fig:chc_spectra_example}.

\textbf{Conclusions:} The integrated framework yields specific,
falsifiable predictions: (1) sensilla-specific wavelength tuning
correlations, (2) IR-only behavioral responses independent of volatile
presence, and (3) sub-10 ms neural responses to electromagnetic
stimulation. Three minimal falsifiers are defined to distinguish
vibrational from purely thermal effects. Validated mechanisms have
immediate applications in biomimetic sensor design and pest management
strategies.

Scope and Reproducibility: Seven case studies are detailed in the
appendices
(\cref{sec:app_sensilla_array,sec:app_environmental_channel,sec:app_detection_limits,sec:app_neural_encoding,sec:app_spectral_unmixing,sec:app_plasmonic_geometry,sec:app_active_inference}).
Computational details, algorithms, and validation tests are presented in
the Appendices and \cref{sec:mathematical_appendix}, consistent with
academic practice of maintaining a narrative style in the main text.
