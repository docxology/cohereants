\section{Appendix B: Environmental Channel
Modeling}\label{sec:app_environmental_channel}

\subsection{Objective}\label{objective}

Comprehensive atmospheric channel modeling validated against
peer-reviewed atmospheric physics: molecular absorption (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄,
O₃), Rayleigh scattering, aerosol effects, channel capacity mapping with
8-14 μm window emphasis, wavelength optimization for 10-100 m ranges,
and environmental sensitivity analysis for IR communication in insect
olfactory systems.

\subsection{Context Note on Biological
Ranges}\label{context-note-on-biological-ranges}

Some insects exhibit sensitivity to thermal IR in natural behaviors
(e.g., Aedes aegypti up to \textasciitilde0.7 m;
\href{https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07848-5}{Chandel et
al.~2024}). These thermal constraints complement our electromagnetic
window analysis and motivate species‑ and wavelength‑specific range
predictions.
