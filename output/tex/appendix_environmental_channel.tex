\section{Appendix B: Environmental Channel
Modeling}\label{sec:app_environmental_channel}

\subsection{Objective}\label{objective}

Comprehensive atmospheric channel modeling: molecular absorption,
Rayleigh scattering, aerosol effects, channel capacity mapping,
wavelength optimization, and environmental sensitivity for IR
communication.

\subsection{Methods (src)}\label{methods-src}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \passthrough{\lstinline!src/case\_studies/environmental\_channel.py!}

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!molecular\_absorption\_cross\_section(wavelengths, molecule\_type)!}
    --- H2O, CO2, CH4 absorption
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!rayleigh\_scattering\_coefficient(wavelengths, air\_density)!}
    --- molecular scattering
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!atmospheric\_transmission\_comprehensive(wavelengths, conditions)!}
    --- multi‑component transmission
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!channel\_capacity\_analysis(wavelengths, environmental\_conditions)!}
    --- Shannon capacity mapping
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!optimize\_wavelength\_for\_range(target\_range, capacity\_requirements)!}
    --- wavelength selection
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!environmental\_sensitivity\_analysis(parameter\_variations)!}
    --- parameter sensitivity
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!atmospheric\_transmission\_detailed(wavelengths, humidity, temperature, path)!}
    --- basic transmission utility
  \item
    \passthrough{\lstinline!channel\_capacity\_vs\_env(material\_props, env\_grid)!}
    --- grid mapping of capacity vs environment
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
